# LinkedIn — founder narrative post

I build AIOS, an open-source toolkit for people and teams working with AI agents. Two
repos, one system: a personal workspace for you, one shared brain for your team.
MIT-licensed, self-hosted. You decide what leaves your machine.

Working with agents on your own is amazing. In a team, it breaks down fast — context
bloats, tools entangle, and the people who've gone deep on AI move at a different
speed than everyone else in the org. I built AIOS because I kept hitting that wall
with the teams I work with, and I wanted a shared structure instead of everyone
reinventing their own.

On Wednesday, August 5, I'm running a small in-person onboarding session in Mas,
Ubud, from 2 to 6pm. You bring a laptop. We set up your AIOS workspace together, and
if you're bringing a team, we get a team brain deployed too. Real setup, not a
slideshow, with food and a few hours somewhere calmer than a conference room.

It's application-gated, not open RSVP — not for exclusivity, but because the
afternoon works best small and hands-on. I review every application myself.

Worth applying if:
— you're part of a technical team already using something like Slack and GitHub,
even a small one
— you run a company and want to move the whole organization forward with AI, not
just add tools around the edges
— you deliver AI-adjacent services yourself and think this could be a fit for people
you work with
— none of the above exactly, but you're seriously diving into AI and think you'd get
real value from an afternoon like this
