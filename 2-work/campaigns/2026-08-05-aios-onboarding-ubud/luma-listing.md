## Full description

I'm John — I build AIOS, an open-source toolkit for people and teams working with AI
agents. It's two repos, one system: a personal workspace for you, one shared brain for
your team. MIT-licensed, self-hosted. You decide what leaves your machine.

I'm running a small, in-person onboarding session in Ubud on **Wednesday, August 5,
2026, from 2:00 to 6:00pm (Bali time)**. The plan is simple: you bring a laptop, we set
up your AIOS workspace together, and if you're working with a team, we get a team brain
deployed too. Real setup, not a slideshow.

This is application-gated, not open RSVP. Not because I want it to feel exclusive —
because the session works best small and hands-on, and I'd rather spend the afternoon
actually getting people set up than running a talk to a room of fifty. Apply below and
I'll follow up personally.

### What to expect

- Hands-on setup of your own AIOS workspace — the numbered folder spine, the
  conventions, the harnesses — on your own machine.
- If you're coming with (or on behalf of) a team already using something like Slack and
  GitHub, we'll get a shared team brain deployed too, so the group has one place to ask
  what's happening instead of ten.
- Direct time with me to talk through where AI actually fits in how you or your team
  work, not a generic AI-strategy pitch.
- Food, a good chair, and a few hours somewhere calmer than a conference room.

### Who this is for

I'm looking for a small, mixed room — self-select into whichever of these sounds like
you:

- You're part of a technical team already using something like Slack and GitHub, even
  a small one — two or three people counts.
- You run a company (roughly 50-150 people) and you want to move the whole
  organization forward with AI, not just add a few AI tools around the edges.
- You deliver AI-adjacent services yourself and might end up referring people this is a
  fit for.
- None of the above fits exactly, but you're seriously diving into AI and think you'd
  get real value out of an afternoon like this — say so in the application.

### How applications work

Fill out the short application below — what you or your team are doing with AI today,
what you're hoping to get out of the afternoon, and a link to your site or LinkedIn so
I can get a sense of fit before I reply. I review every application myself and follow
up directly, whether that's a yes, a "not quite yet," or a redirect to something that
fits better. Seats are limited on purpose.

### Logistics

- **When:** Wednesday, August 5, 2026, 2:00-6:00pm WITA (Bali time).
- **Where:** Mas, Ubud, Bali. The exact address goes out privately once your
  application is accepted.
- **What to bring:** a laptop, and admin access to whatever accounts you'll need to set
  up your workspace (GitHub at minimum; Slack if your team uses it).
- **Cost:** free. Food's covered.
